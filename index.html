About

I'm a Salesforce Consultant with 10+ years working across Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and CPQ — the person teams bring in because I move fluidly across whatever the platform actually needs, not just one lane. On a given engagement I might run requirements discovery like an analyst, configure and secure the org like an admin, write Apex triggers and SOQL alongside engineers, or own the roadmap and prioritization like a product owner. The title changes; the ownership doesn't.

My work sits across the full Quote-to-Cash lifecycle: requirements workshops, gap analysis, CPQ solution design (bundles, price rules, approvals), integration analysis, and UAT/SIT coordination with Sales, Finance, and Revenue Operations. I currently own requirements intake and solution design for a platform supporting 1,500+ Sales Cloud and Service Cloud users, plus 100+ community users.

Titles like "Systems Analyst" and "Administrator" undersell how much of this work is genuinely cross-functional — I'm usually the person in the room translating between engineering, sales leadership, and finance, and I stay hands-on with configuration rather than handing it off.
